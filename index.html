I am a PhD scholar at IIT Delhi, under supervision of Prof. Piyush Kumar Sharma, where I study how anonymous communication systems like Mixnets, TOR and VPNs can be attacked, measured and audited. I came to this area from quantum machine learning, cryptography and image processing, which shaped how I think about data security.

04Past Research Projects

[1]2026
Quantum Encoding for Image Compression
With Dr. Om Pal, University of Delhi completed

Developed a hybrid method that merges classical compression with quantum state mapping, to cut the qubit cost of the FRQI and NEQR image encodings.

[2]Fall 2025
Evaluating Hybrid Quantum-Classical CNNs
With Prof. Neelima Gupta and Dr. Om Pal, University of Delhi completed

Compared pure, hybrid and multi-branch quantum convolutional networks on MNIST and COVID-19 radiography. [report]

[3]Spring 2025
Deep Learning Image Steganography
Team project, University of Delhi completed

An encoder-decoder CNN, trained on Tiny ImageNet, that hides a secret image inside a cover image. [report]

[4]Fall 2024
Hybrid Movie Recommendation System
Team project, University of Delhi completed

Moved from an SVD baseline (NDCG 0.81) to Neural Collaborative Filtering combining GMF and an MLP (NDCG 0.89), addressing data sparsity and cold start. [report]

[5]Spring 2024
Automated Legal Document Summarizer
With Dr. Anshul Verma, Banaras Hindu University completed

Compared Legal Pegasus, BART and the Longformer Encoder-Decoder on Indian and UK Supreme Court cases.
